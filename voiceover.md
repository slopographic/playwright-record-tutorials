# Slop PDF walkthrough: voiceover script

Video: `slop-pdf-walkthrough.mp4` (about 187 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps). Times are seconds from the start of the video, taken from `steps.json`.

**ElevenLabs steps**
1. Create a voice project in ElevenLabs (Projects) and paste in the narration blocks below, one block per clip.
2. Generate each block on its own, so each clip can be placed separately.
3. In your video editor, put each clip on the timeline at its start time (the number after the block name). Each clip should finish before the next start time.

---

## Intro · 0.0 s
**On screen:** the empty "Drop PDFs here" screen (before any caption).
**Narration:** "Meet Slop PDF, a PDF tool that runs right in your browser."

## 1 · 5.6 s: Open Slop PDF and show the empty state
**On screen:** the empty state, with the cursor moving over the drop area and the privacy note.
**Narration:** "Nothing is uploaded. Your files never leave your device, and there is no account to set up."

## 2 · 14.4 s: Open the sample PDF
**On screen:** a sample PDF loads and its two pages appear.
**Narration:** "Here we open a sample PDF."

## 3 · 19.2 s: Open a page and zoom in
**On screen:** a page opens in the page view, and the zoom is turned up twice.
**Narration:** "Open a page to see it up close, then zoom in to read the small print."

## 4 · 39.4 s: Select a line of text and press Highlight
**On screen:** a line of text is selected, the selection bar appears, and Highlight is pressed.
**Narration:** "Select a line of text and a small toolbar appears. Press Highlight to mark it."

## 5 · 57.7 s: Edit text: double-click a word, type a new one, press Enter
**On screen:** Edit text is turned on, a word is double-clicked, the new word is typed, and Enter is pressed.
**Narration:** "To change text, turn on Edit text, double-click a word, type the new word and press Enter."

## 6 · 69.0 s: Open the Fonts panel from the top bar
**On screen:** the Fonts dialog opens from the top bar, then closes.
**Narration:** "The Fonts button shows which fonts this document uses."

## 7 · 76.6 s: Open the Convert panel and show Word, Excel and PowerPoint
**On screen:** the Convert panel opens from the tools rail, and the cursor rests on the Word, Excel and PowerPoint buttons.
**Narration:** "Convert turns a PDF into Word, Excel or PowerPoint files. It can also make images of each page."

## 8 · 101.1 s: Show the Tools rail and open OCR
**On screen:** the cursor moves along the tools rail, and the OCR panel opens.
**Narration:** "The Tools rail holds the rest of the tools. OCR reads scanned pages on this device."

## 9 · 110.7 s: Open the Layers panel
**On screen:** the Edit page panel opens, and the Layers list comes into view.
**Narration:** "The Layers panel lists the layers on this page. You can hide, lock or reorder each one."

## 10 · 121.0 s: Organize: drag a page to a new position
**On screen:** the Organize tool shows the page thumbnails, the first page is dragged to a new position with the cursor, and the new page order is shown.
**Narration:** "Organize shows every page as a thumbnail. Drag a page to a new spot, and the order updates at once."

## 11 · 144.0 s: Redact a word: Redact in the selection bar, then Apply redactions
**On screen:** a word is selected, Redact is pressed in the selection bar, Apply redactions is confirmed in the Redact tool, and the word is blacked out.
**Narration:** "To remove text for good, select it and press Redact. Then Apply redactions, and the word is blacked out for good."

## Outro · 181.7 s
**On screen:** the redacted page, held until the end of the video.
**Narration:** "That's Slop PDF. It runs in your browser, and your files stay on your device."
